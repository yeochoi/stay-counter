CountdownRing

The hero of Today. One number in a ring, one labelled date beside it.

  • `size-ring` circle on `hero`. A faint white track always shows, so every state looks like the same component.
  • During a stay the arc fills in `ring`, showing how much of the stay is left. The number is days left, counted from tomorrow.
  • Before a trip, or on a day between stays, there is no arc, only the track, with days to go.
  • The number and the date both use `answer`, the unit and the label both use `body`, so the tops and bottoms line up.
  • Nothing else in the hero during a stay. No stay count, no next entry chip.

StayTimeline

The stays of the trip you are on, on Today. Answers where am I in this trip at a glance.

  • A section label with the country, like China trip, and the whole trip on the right, like Sep 8 – Nov 17 · 69 days.
  • One row per stay. The dates in `headline`, a status line in `note` under it, the days on the right.
  • Status says what each stay is. Done, Now with days left, Back in N days, Suggested, or Matched to flights.
  • Dots match. `done-dot` for done, a filled `primary` dot with a `current-line` halo for now, an empty `primary` ring for next. The now row sits on `current`.
  • A stay past the end of visa free or a visa adds a coral line, After visa free ends.
  • Tapping a stay opens Stay N of M to match it to flights. Later stays move along.

ListRow

A trip in a list. On phones two lines, on laptops one table row.

  • Phones. Country in `headline`, then one `subline` in `muted` with dates, days in the country and type, like Sep 26 – Oct 2 · 7 days · Personal. Planning is added for future trips not yet booked.
  • Laptops. One row with aligned columns for country, dates, days and type, with no header row. The last column has a fixed width so rows line up across cards.
  • The trip you are in sits in its own card on `current` with a `current-line` border and a Now chip with days left.
  • Every row has a chevron and opens the trip overview.
  • Trips is split by tabs, Upcoming and Past. Past groups by year.

TaxDays

Days in each country, counted the way that country counts for tax.

  • One row per country visited, home left out.
  • The second line says the window. In 2026 for a calendar year rule like China, Past 12 months for a rolling rule.
  • Small counts show only the days. From 60 days a bar appears with N of 183 days. Close to the limit the value becomes a coral chip and the bar turns coral.
  • Counts run up to today, so they match what has really happened.

Warning

How the app says something needs checking. One style everywhere.

  • One icon per screen, inside a chip under the answer. The chip is `coral-soft` with `note` text in `coral-text`, like 2 rules to check. See below.
  • Every other warning is one `note` line in `coral-text` directly under the thing it is about, with no icon. After visa free ends under a stay, Your trip goes past this date under Visa free until.
  • The value in the row keeps its normal `headline` ink. Only the line under it is coral.
  • Calm words. Rules to check, never broken.
  • A new trip's plan before saving has no rules card, so its warnings sit in one coral box instead.

Chip

Small rounded labels.

  • On `hero`, chips are white at 10 percent with white `note` text, like 147 days, Business, Visa free.
  • In content, Now is `primary` with white text, the close to limit chip is `coral-soft` with `coral-text`.
  • Every chip uses regular weight. Its fill already makes it stand out.
  • Chips never stack into a second line. If they would, drop the least useful one.

NoteBox

A quiet grey box that explains why a screen behaves differently.

  • `chip` background, `chip-text` in `note`, `radius-note`, 12 by 14 padding.
  • Used on a finished stay, This stay is done. Change the dates only if you arrived or left on a different day.
  • For context only. Never for warnings, those are coral lines.

SheetBar

The top of every sheet. Leaving always lives here.

  • Three columns. Left a round `size-nav-button` on `line`, center the title in `headline`, right an optional text action in `purple-text`, like Edit.
  • × closes a first screen, like New trip, Settings or a trip overview.
  • ‹ goes back one step inside a flow, like Your visa, the plan, Edit trip, or Design system from Settings.
  • The bottom of a sheet holds only the forward action. Reading pages have no bottom button.
  • Titles never repeat the content below. A saved trip's form is Edit trip, not the country. A stay in a split trip is Stay 2 of 5.

GroupedRows

Label and value rows in a white card, for forms, rules and details.

  • `surface` card, 1px `line` border, `radius-card`, rows `size-row` tall with a `line` divider.
  • Label on the left in `body` `muted`, value on the right in `headline` `ink`.
  • A `chevron` when the row opens a picker or another step. Read only rows, like Rules for this trip, have no chevron.
  • An empty field shows Choose in `muted` regular, never a made up value.

SegmentedSwitch

A two choice switch, like Planning or Booked, Business or Personal.

  • Track in `line`, thumb in `surface` with `shadow-segment`, both `radius-pill`.
  • Labels in `body`. The selected one is semibold `purple-text`, the other `muted`.
  • In a new trip nothing is selected until the person taps.

Button

The one forward action at the bottom of a sheet or screen, full width.

  • Primary fills `primary` with white `headline` text, `size-button` tall, `radius-pill`.
  • Soft fills `soft` with `purple-text`. Only used as the second choice on the empty Today screen, like Add booked trip.
  • One primary per screen. No Cancel or Back buttons at the bottom, leaving lives at the top left.
  • Disabled is the primary at 45 percent opacity.
  • Labels say what happens, like See stays, Save trip or Save.

TabBar

Today and Trips.

  • Phones. A bottom bar on `bg` with a `line` on top. The selected tab has a filled icon in `primary` and a `caption` label in `purple-text`. The other is an outline icon in `muted`.
  • Laptops. The tabs move to a top bar on `hero`, text only, with Stay Counter in `section` caps on the left. The selected tab is white with a `ring` underline.
